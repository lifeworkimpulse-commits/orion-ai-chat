# Grounded shopping answers

Version 0.10.3 tightens customer-facing product responses.

- Product volume or pack size is not evidence that the quantity is appropriate for the project area.
- Quantity and coverage may only be stated when explicit numeric manufacturer coverage is supplied with the verified product data.
- The model must not cite its own recommendation as verification.
- Missing application tools are presented briefly.
- Primer, filler, sandpaper, scraper and cleaner are described as conditional preparation items, not universally required products.
- Missing optional roles do not suppress a verified principal material or create a manager handoff.
